Privacy Policy

Draft website policy for AmorCheck LLC. Review with qualified counsel before public launch.

1. Who we are

AmorCheck LLC provides confidential relationship verification and investigation-support services. Public contact: contact@amorcheck.com and +1 860 555 6688.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the service, we may receive contact information, case descriptions, names or aliases, public profile links, screenshots, photographs, documents, payment-related records, correspondence and other information voluntarily provided by a client or story submitter.

3. Why we use information

We use information to assess service requests, perform agreed verification work, communicate with clients, prepare reports, prevent abuse, process payments, comply with legal obligations and operate the website.

4. Data minimisation

Please submit only information that is relevant to the purpose of your request and that you are legally permitted to provide. AmorCheck may decline or delete material that is unnecessary, unlawful or excessively sensitive.

5. Story submissions

Story submissions are private by default. Submission does not grant automatic permission to publish. If AmorCheck wishes to publish an anonymized account, separate approval should be obtained before publication.

6. Service providers

We may use service providers for hosting, secure forms, file storage, communications, analytics and payment processing. Those providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

7. International transfers

AmorCheck LLC is a United States company and serves European clients. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers of personal data.

8. Retention

Case information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legal obligations, dispute handling and legitimate security needs. A final production policy should state the exact retention periods used by the company.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect submitted information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and to request a copy of certain personal information.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@amorcheck.com.

This draft intentionally avoids publishing a private residential or investigator address. Before launch, confirm what business-contact and representative information must legally be disclosed in the countries where AmorCheck offers services.
